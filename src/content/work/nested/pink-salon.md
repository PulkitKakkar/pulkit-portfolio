Pink Beauty Salon needed a clearer digital journey for customers exploring treatments, products, and consultation options. The goal was to make the site easier to navigate, easier to maintain, and more useful for appointment intake.

## Role

I led the technical design and development of the Shopify experience, covering site structure, theme customisation, service content, product presentation, and the booking flow. I also designed an AI-assisted consultation journey using OpenAI's GPT models to help capture customer preferences before an appointment.

## Key Areas

- Designing a Shopify storefront that supports services, products, and consultation journeys
- Building an AI-assisted booking and intake flow using OpenAI's GPT models
- Structuring service pages, headers, footers, and navigation for usability and SEO
- Keeping the experience simple for staff to manage and customers to complete
- Creating a foundation that can evolve as the salon adds services and offers

## Impact

The result is a cleaner customer journey, a more structured appointment intake flow, stronger organic visibility, and a maintainable Shopify setup the business can continue to improve without heavy technical overhead.
