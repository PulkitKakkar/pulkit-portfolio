Almighty Musicals needed a wholesale-focused platform for Indian musical instruments, aimed at music stores, event organisers, and international distributors. A standard retail checkout was not the right fit because customers often need bulk pricing, stock clarity, and a quote-led purchasing process.

## Role

I designed and developed the platform using **React**, **Vite**, and **Firebase**, focusing on a maintainable frontend architecture, clear data flow, and workflows that support B2B enquiries instead of one-off consumer purchases.

## Key Areas

- Building dynamic inventory listings for wholesale product discovery
- Designing a quote-led enquiry flow instead of a traditional checkout
- Creating admin-friendly tools for stock, order, and customer communication
- Supporting supplier order management and traceability requirements
- Keeping the platform fast, simple, and easy to extend as the catalogue grows

## Impact

Almighty Musicals now has a scalable foundation for wholesale operations, with clearer product discovery, better enquiry handling, and a structure that can support future inventory and supplier workflows. The website is ready to launch and is currently hosted at [main.d2zwc2np4txide.amplifyapp.com](https://main.d2zwc2np4txide.amplifyapp.com/), awaiting final inputs from the client.

## Outcome Signals

- Replaced a generic retail mindset with a quote-led wholesale journey
- Made product discovery easier for bulk buyers and distributors
- Created a structure that can scale as catalogue and supplier workflows grow
- Kept the technical setup lightweight enough for a small business to maintain
